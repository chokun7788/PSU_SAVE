Website Security Policy – PSU Esports Studio Phuket Reservation System
Website Security Policy
Security Measures and Practices
PSU Esports Studio – Phuket recognizes the importance of website security in order to protect users’ information from unauthorized access, destruction, or malicious intrusion. We have therefore established website security measures by implementing advanced information security standards, including Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology with 128-bit encryption or higher.
This ensures that all data transmitted through the internet during interactions with our Website is encrypted and cannot be intercepted or misused by unauthorized parties. Users can confirm secure access by checking for the “https://” prefix in the website URL.
Additional Security Technologies
In addition to the general measures mentioned above, PSU Esports Studio – Phuket employs the following advanced technologies to safeguard your personal information:
Firewall – A security system that restricts access to authorized personnel or approved systems only.
Virus Scanning – All service computers are equipped with high-performance antivirus software that is regularly updated. Additionally, specialized virus scanning software is installed on our servers and network systems.
Cookies – Our Website may use cookies to temporarily store necessary data on users’ devices for improved efficiency and communication. However, PSU Esports Studio – Phuket respects user privacy and avoids the use of cookies unless absolutely necessary. If cookies are used, we ensure they are handled with the highest regard for security and confidentiality.
Security Recommendations for Users
While PSU Esports Studio – Phuket implements industry-standard technologies and practices to protect user data, it is important to note that no system can guarantee absolute protection against unauthorized access or malicious attacks. Therefore, users are encouraged to follow these best practices to enhance their own security:
Exercise caution when downloading programs from the internet and verify website addresses before logging in, to avoid phishing or website spoofing.
Install and regularly update antivirus software on personal devices.
Use a personal firewall to protect your computer from unauthorized access attempts by hackers or other malicious actors.

Privacy Policy – PSU Esports Studio Phuket Reservation System
Privacy Policy and Personal Data Protection
This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how PSU Esports Studio – Phuket, Prince of Songkla University, Phuket Campus collects, uses, and discloses personal data (“Personal Data”) that you (“User” or “you”) provide when using the website of PSU Esports Studio – Phuket, Prince of Songkla University, Phuket Campus at
https://esports.computing.psu.ac.th
and its related products or services (collectively referred to as the “Website” or “Services”), in compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) (“PDPA”).
This Policy also describes the options available to you regarding the use of your Personal Data and how you can access or update such information. This Policy does not apply to the practices of companies or organizations not managed by us, or individuals we do not employ or manage.
Information We Collect Automatically
When you visit the PSU Esports Studio – Phuket, Prince of Songkla University, Phuket Campus Website, our servers automatically record information that your browser sends. Such information may include your device’s IP address, browser type and version, operating system type and version, the webpage you visited before accessing the PSU Esports Studio – Phuket, Prince of Songkla University, Phuket Campus Website, the pages of our Website you visited, the time spent on those pages, the information you searched for on our Website, the date and time of your visit, and other statistics.
Automatically collected information is used to identify potential violations and to generate statistical data regarding the usage of the PSU Esports Studio – Phuket, Prince of Songkla University, Phuket Campus Website. Such statistical data is not collected in a way that would identify any particular user of the system.
